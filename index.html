Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device, and — if you turn on iCloud sync — in your own private iCloud account. Data read from Apple Health always stays on your device and is never synced. There's no Ventari account and no server; we never see your data.

Does it work with Apple Health?
Optionally, yes. If you connect Health, Ventari reads data like sleep and activity to add context. It never writes to or changes your Health data.

Is this medical advice?
No. Ventari highlights patterns that may be worth discussing with a clinician — never a diagnosis. If your symptoms are severe, persistent, or worsening, please seek medical care.

Can I lock the app?
Yes. In Settings › Privacy, lock Ventari with Face ID and/or a 4-digit PIN. Its content is also hidden in the app switcher.

How do I remove my data?
In the app, go to Settings › Delete all my data. That erases everything you've recorded, including the synced copy if iCloud sync is on. Deleting the app also removes on-device data.
