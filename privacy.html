Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Ventari ("the app," "we") helps you track what you eat and how your gut feels, spot patterns over time, and bring a clear record to your doctor. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

The short version: your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. We have no server, no account system, and no analytics. We never see your data, and we never sell or share it.

Who is responsible

Ventari is developed by an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact us at roostoniphone@gmail.com.

We have no account and no server of our own

Ventari does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number. There is no Ventari server. We do not operate a backend that receives your health information, and we cannot access what you record.

What information the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — bowel-movement counts, symptom ratings (such as abdominal pain, bloating, and urgency), foods and ingredients, medications and supplements, lifestyle notes (such as sleep quality, stress, alcohol, and caffeine), elimination trials, reminder preferences, and app settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database.

If you leave iCloud sync enabled (you can turn it off in Settings), that same information is also stored in your own private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so it can sync between your Apple devices. This data lives in your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms; it is not visible to us and is not sent to any third party. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how iCloud handles data.

Apple Health (HealthKit) data

If — and only if — you choose to connect Apple Health, Ventari reads the following data types to give your symptom patterns more context. It reads them; it never writes, changes, or deletes anything in Apple Health.

  • Requested when you connect: steps, active energy burned, sleep, and workouts.
  • Requested later, only if a related feature needs it: resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, body mass, mindful minutes, and menstrual flow.

Health data read by the app is used solely on your device to display and analyse your own trends. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties or disclosed to us. Data read from Apple Health is kept only on your device. It is never copied to iCloud, even when iCloud sync is on; on a new device, the app reads it from Apple Health again. You can revoke the app's Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, a morning check-in) are local notifications scheduled on your device from the times you choose in Settings. They are not push notifications, involve no server, and send no information anywhere. You can disable them in the app or in iOS Settings.

Exporting and sharing your data — your choice

Ventari can generate a CSV file and a PDF clinical summary so you can share your record with your doctor. These are created on demand and handed to the standard iOS share sheet, where you decide what to do with them (AirDrop, email, save to Files, etc.). The app does not upload or transmit these files on its own.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, marketing, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health data.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Your controls

  • Turn off iCloud sync in Settings to keep data on-device only.
  • Revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Turn off reminders in the app or iOS Settings.
  • Lock the app with Face ID and/or a 4-digit PIN in Settings → Privacy. The PIN is stored only as a one-way hash in your device's Keychain.
  • Delete your data in Settings → Delete all my data. This erases everything you have recorded (and, if iCloud sync is on, the synced copy too). Deleting the app also removes on-device data.

Children

Ventari is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

Ventari is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Anything it surfaces is a hypothesis to discuss with a clinician, never a conclusion. If you have severe, persistent, or worsening symptoms, seek medical care.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on the App Store product page and in the app.
