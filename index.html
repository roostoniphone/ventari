Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device, and — if you leave iCloud sync on — in your own private iCloud account. There's no Ventari account and no server; we never see your data.

Does it work with Apple Health?
Optionally, yes. If you connect Health, Ventari reads data like sleep and activity to add context. It never writes to or changes your Health data.

Is this medical advice?
No. Ventari highlights patterns that may be worth discussing with a clinician — never a diagnosis. If your symptoms are severe, persistent, or worsening, please seek medical care.

How do I remove my data?
Delete the app to remove on-device data. To also remove synced data, delete Ventari's data from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.
